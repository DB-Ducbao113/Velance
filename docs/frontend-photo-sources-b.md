# VÉLANCE — photographic archive B

Real-car photographs selected from Wikimedia Commons and visually checked against the intended model and variant. The photographed colours and individual configurations are independent of the interactive GLB renders. No generated images, rendered previews, prototypes of the 918, or unverified candidates are included.

Source records below use Commons imageinfo published JPEG renditions (requested 960, 1600 or 3840px; decoded sizes were 960, 1920 or 3840px). The initial acquisition covered six 3840px leads; the subsequent priority follow-up below adds a verified 4K 765LT and qualifying Valkyrie lead while preserving all initial photographs. Original dimensions and SHA-1 are metadata only; original bytes were not downloaded. The retrieved rendition width and aspect ratio were verified and its bytes hashed. Derivatives preserve framing with EXIF orientation correction, RGB conversion, proportional Lanczos downsampling and WebP encoding; no upscaling, cropping, grading or compositing. Full means largest verified local rendition, not original camera resolution.

Display author credit, source and license links with each photograph and a resized/WebP-encoded modification notice. CC BY-SA derivatives retain the listed share-alike license. No claim is made about vehicle trademarks or other non-copyright rights.
