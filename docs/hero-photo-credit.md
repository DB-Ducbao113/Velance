# Archived landing hero photograph — McLaren P1

**Historical asset:** this photograph is retained locally but is no longer displayed
by the current Portal landing. See `docs/frontend-portal.md` for the active hero.

The landing uses **one intact real-world photograph** of the McLaren P1. Its wheels, windows, reflections, original environment and ground contact are preserved. There is no synthetic car, 3D model, foreground segmentation, digitally rotating wheel, or composited replacement studio.

- **Subject:** McLaren P1, Volcano Orange, photographed at night.
- **Photographer:** Axion23.
- **Original file:** [Volcano Orange McLaren P1 (18852267050).jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Volcano_Orange_McLaren_P1_(18852267050).jpg).
- **Original Flickr listing:** [Axion23 — Volcano Orange McLaren P1](https://www.flickr.com/photos/gfreeman23/18852267050/).
- **License:** [Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 Generic (CC BY 2.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). Redistribution, including edits, requires attribution, a license link and an indication that edits were made.
- **Original dimensions:** 5,763 × 3,842 px (JPEG); SHA-256 `d602ddd7c6fbd3f69ee2b2dbb02e16c12cbb0ea43403a357c1d76c021008ddc6`.
- **Changes:** desktop framing/crop at 3,200 × 2,000, slight contrast and sharpening adjustments and WebP encoding. The page uses only CSS dark/champagne overlays and a subtle transform of the **entire photograph**. All car parts and their physical relation to the scene are untouched.
- **Website attribution:** small `PHOTO · AXION23 / CC BY 2.0` links at the bottom of the entrance page.
